Reaction sounds
===============
clap.mp3  — trimmed from "Clapping hurray (cropped).oga"
            Wikimedia Commons · Public domain
            https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Clapping_hurray_(cropped).oga

laugh.mp3 — trimmed from "Laughter and clearing voice.ogg"
            Wikimedia Commons · Public domain
            https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Laughter_and_clearing_voice.ogg

Both sources are public domain. Clips were trimmed, faded and loudness-
normalised (-18 LUFS) for use as short in-call reaction cues.
All other reaction sounds are synthesized at runtime (see js/app.js).
