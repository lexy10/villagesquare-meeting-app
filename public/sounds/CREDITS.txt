Reaction sounds
===============
clap.mp3  — crowd applause, played when two or more people clap together.
            Mix of two recordings, both CC0 (public domain dedication):
            "applause-2" by sandermotions
            https://freesound.org/s/277021/
            "crowd partying cheering applause all around" by kyles
            https://freesound.org/s/637468/

laugh.mp3 — trimmed from "Laughter and clearing voice.ogg"
            Wikimedia Commons · Public domain
            https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Laughter_and_clearing_voice.ogg

Clips were trimmed, faded and loudness-normalised (-18 LUFS) for use as short
in-call reaction cues. All other sounds (join/leave chimes, the other reactions,
the raised-hand talking drum, the photo shutter) are synthesized at runtime in
src/lib/sounds.ts.
